## Multi Compare Overview

One session compares many images in a layout grid with per-slot drops and focus mode.

### Open a session {#open-session}

{{tr:workspace.session_types.multi_compare}} in the Session Picker or {{tr:action.workspace.new_multi_compare}} via {{tr:menu.find_action}} (`Ctrl+Shift+P`). See [Workspace Tabs](help://workspace.session_picker).

### What you can do {#capability-map}

- [Layouts and slots](help://workspace.multi_compare.layouts) — cells, gap splits, dividers, actions.
- [Viewing and focus](help://workspace.multi_compare.viewing) — focus, zoom, pan, labels, crop.
- [Save and export](help://workspace.multi_compare.export) — quick save, save dialog, sessions.
