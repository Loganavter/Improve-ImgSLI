## Multi Compare Overview

Multi Compare compares three or more images at once. It arranges them in a grid
of slots that you can focus, zoom, pan, crop, and save together. Use it when a
two-image split is too narrow — for example, to scan a whole series side by side
or to compare several variants of one shot.

### Open a session {#open-session}

1. Open the **Session Picker** and choose the **Multi Compare** card, or press
   `Ctrl+Shift+P` and run **New Multi Compare Session** in **Find Action**.
2. Add images with `Ctrl+O` (**Add Images**), by dropping files from the file
   manager onto the grid, or by pasting from the clipboard.
3. Drag a divider to resize the slots; every new image splits the largest cell
   along its longer side.

See [Session Picker](help://workspace.session_picker) for the full list of
session types and recent projects.

### What you can do {#capability-map}

The session keeps your grid as you work, so you can load a batch, compare it, and
save the result without leaving the tab.

- [Multi Compare Layouts](help://workspace.multi_compare.layouts) — how slots fill, how a gap drop splits a cell, and how dividers resize it.
- [Multi Compare Viewing](help://workspace.multi_compare.viewing) — focus one slot, share zoom and pan, style labels, and crop each slot.
- [Multi Compare Export](help://workspace.multi_compare.export) — quick save or the save dialog, and what a session remembers.
