## Multi Compare Viewing

Viewing covers focus mode, shared zoom and pan, labels, and per-slot crop. These controls change only how the grid is displayed; they do not alter the source files.

### Focus mode {#focus-mode}

Click a slot to focus it. A press that turns into a drag moves the slot instead. Click the dimmed area or press `Esc` to leave focus. Zoom and pan keep working while a slot is focused.

### Zoom and pan {#zoom-and-pan}

- **Shared view** — zoom and pan apply to the whole grid, not to each slot separately.
- **Wheel** — zooms from 1.0x to 50x in 1.1x steps; the point under the cursor stays in place.
- **Pan limits** — panning stops at the edge of the image; at fit size or smaller the view snaps back.
- **Middle-drag** — drag with the middle mouse button to pan.
- **`0`** — resets zoom and pan.

### Keyboard {#keyboard}

The shortcuts for this tab are listed in [Hotkeys](help://platform.hotkeys).

### Grid and labels {#grid-and-labels}

Divider settings are described under [Multi Compare Layouts](help://workspace.multi_compare.layouts#dividers). **Label Settings** opens a panel where you style the slot label text.

### Slot crop {#slot-crop}

Crop removes black borders from a slot's image. Each slot's crop is either on, off, or follows the global auto-crop setting. A slot that follows the global setting changes when that setting changes; a slot set to on or off keeps its choice. An empty slot has nothing to crop. The crop applies to the grid and to saved images.
