## Multi Compare Viewing

Viewing covers focus mode, shared zoom and pan, labels, and per-slot crop.

### Focus mode {#focus-mode}

A single click on a slot focuses it. A press that turns into a drag moves the slot instead. A click on the dimmed area or `Esc` leaves focus. Zoom and pan work inside focus.

### Zoom and pan {#zoom-and-pan}

- **Shared view** — zoom and pan apply to the whole grid, not to each slot.
- **Wheel** — zoom from 1.0x to 50x in 1.1x steps; the point under the cursor stays in place.
- **Pan limits** — panning stops at the edge of the image. At fit size or smaller, the view snaps back.
- **Middle-drag** — drag with the middle mouse button to pan.
- **`0`** — resets zoom and pan.

### Keyboard {#keyboard}

The shortcuts for this tab are listed in [Hotkeys](help://hotkeys).

### Grid and labels {#grid-and-labels}

Divider settings are described under [Dividers](help://mc_layouts#dividers). Label text is styled through {{tr:multi_compare.action.text_settings}}.

### Slot crop {#slot-crop}

Crop removes black borders from a slot's image. Each slot's crop is either on, off, or follows the global auto-crop setting. A slot that follows the global setting changes when that setting changes; a slot set to on or off keeps its choice. An empty slot has nothing to crop. The crop applies to the grid and to saved images.
