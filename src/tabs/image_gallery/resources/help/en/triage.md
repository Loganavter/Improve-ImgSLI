## Triage and Review

Triage keeps one winner per series and can move the rejected images out of the folder.

### Keep best of series {#keep-best}

- **Keep best** — keeps the best image of every series and rejects the rest.
- The keeper stays at rank 1 in its series; the rejected cards dim and get a strike-through.
- **Show all** — clears every rejected flag and brings the images back.

### Make best and reject {#make-best-reject}

- **Make best** — marks the selected image as the winner of its series.
- **Reject** — toggles the rejected flag on the selected images; when all of them are rejected, the label flips to **Unreject**.
- **Remove from view** — `Space` or `Delete` takes the selected cards out of the grid; the files stay on disk.

### Clean review {#clean-review}

- **Clean rejected** — moves the rejected images into the `_rejected/` folder beside them. A preview of the move appears first, and the files stay on disk.
- **Undo** — in the toast that follows the move, restores the moved files to the folder.

### Run triage again {#run-triage}

**Run triage** reopens the current folder and groups the images again from scratch.

### Set as source {#set-as-source}

**Set as source** in **Quick Look** and **Find Action** — or **Make source** on a card — chooses the image used as the comparison reference. The reference is always picked by hand. See [Quality Metrics](help://workspace.image_gallery.quality#reference-source).
